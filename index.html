About

I'm a second-year Cyber Security undergraduate focused on the investigative side of the field — digital forensics, incident analysis, and understanding exactly how systems break. I learn by doing: CTFs, labs and projects I can point to, rather than theory alone.

My day-to-day is hands-on. Carving files out of disk images, reading registry hives, walking packet captures frame by frame, and documenting what I find in a way another examiner could follow. Write-ups are how I make sure I actually understood something.
